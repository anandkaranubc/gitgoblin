# Excluded projects

Maintainers who do not want gitgoblin-assisted contributions can ask to be listed here; [etiquette.md](etiquette.md) explains the commitments behind this. Being listed means gitgoblin users must not open pull requests, issues, or comments in the project, and gitgoblin's tools are expected to refuse to.

To be added or removed, fill in the [maintainer form](https://github.com/anandkaranubc/gitgoblin/issues/new?template=maintainer_report.yml). No reason is needed.

Entries are one per line as `host/owner/name`, for example `github.com/example/widgets`. An entry without a name, such as `github.com/example`, excludes everything that owner has.
